Urban Agriculture & Food Security

Harvest of Hope Community Garden

Situated in a recognized East Mesa food desert, our community garden offers raised garden beds for rent, education on healthy eating, organic gardening techniques, and fresh produce. Join us in cultivating green spaces and neighbors!

Campus Location: East Campus Grounds Beds: Available for public rental

Shelter & Compassion

I-HELP (Interfaith Homeless Lodging)

In partnership with Lutheran Social Services of the Southwest, Bethlehem opens our doors as an emergency overnight shelter for homeless women in Mesa on 5th Fridays. Our volunteers prepare warm meals, offer hospitality, and ensure a safe night's sleep.

When: 5th Friday of applicable months Partner: Lutheran Social Services of the SW
Volunteer with I-HELP
Hunger Relief

Bethie Lu's Pantry & ELCA World Hunger

The Pantry Corner: Each month our church collects designated food staples (soups, proteins, cereals, paper goods) delivered directly to the First Evangelical Lutheran Church community food pantry.

ELCA World Hunger: Through Sunday hunger collections and our annual Souper Bowl fundraiser, Bethlehem sends relief locally and internationally to combat hunger and poverty.

Collection: Food cart located weekly in the Narthex
