About Bethlehem

How we are structured and work together to accomplish our mission.

Bethlehem Lutheran Church is a non-profit Arizona corporation. We are tax-exempt under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code because of our association with the Evangelical Lutheran Church in America (ELCA). Ultimately, the Church exists to serve, honor, and glorify God and to help the members and friends of Bethlehem Lutheran Church grow into a closer relationship with Jesus Christ.

We govern ourselves democratically, electing members of the Congregation Council who oversee the mission and ministry of the church. The Articles of Incorporation, Constitution, and Bylaws describe the specific areas of responsibility and authority for each group of governance.

Bethlehem Lutheran Church is a staff and team led and managed church. Our professional staff operates the various ministries within guidelines established by our policy, procedure, and governing documents. The staff is also responsible for the ongoing development of our ministries as well as strategic planning.

Our History

A journey of faith, community, and service in Mesa from 1986 to today.

1986 – 1988

Founding of Bethlehem

Pastor Stephen and Estelle Knudsen began worship gatherings at the Seventh Day Adventist Church in Mesa. On February 16, 1988, Bethlehem was officially organized as a congregation with 98 charter members.

1990

First Campus Building Built

Bethlehem constructed its first permanent unit of 7,500 square feet, providing worship access, fellowship hall, kitchen, and education classrooms. The four-acre site accommodated both year-round and winter visitor members. Pastor Maynard Midthun served as visitation pastor and Kris Anderson as the church's first intern.

1995 – 1998

Expansion & Education Facility

Pastor Dale Olson was called as Senior Pastor in 1996 following Pastor Adam Meidinger's interim ministry. In 1997, Bethlehem stepped out in faith to purchase an additional four acres, bringing the campus to eight acres total. In 1998, as more children filled the classrooms, Bethlehem built a new 2,400-square-foot educational and preschool building.

2000

Noah's Ark Preschool Opens

September 2000 marked an exciting chapter with the opening of Noah’s Ark Preschool under the direction of Jan Wilton with 36 children enrolled. Pat Servi served as intern.

2002 – 2009

Outreach, Music & Service

Pastor Terry Pletkovich arrived in 2004, serving Bethlehem alongside his duty as a Navy Reserve Chaplain. In 2006, Pastor Ryan Rouse assisted as Interim Pastor during Pastor Terry's active service recall. Noah's Ark children performed at the National ELCA Conference and sang the National Anthem on television at Chase Field for the Arizona Diamondbacks Faith & Family Day.

2010 – 2018

25th Anniversary, Community Garden & Stephen Ministry

Pastor Chon Pugh began her call as permanent pastor in 2011, and Jill Majestic became permanent director of Noah’s Ark Preschool. In 2012, Bethlehem celebrated its 25th Anniversary, established the Harvest of Hope Community Garden, and installed its first Stephen Ministry class. Deaconess Elyse Wheeler completed internship and consecration during these fruitful years of ministry.

2019 – Present

New Call & Continuing Grace

Following faithful years of ministry by Pastor Chon Pugh and interim guidance by Pastor John Schaumburg, the congregation joyfully voted on June 27, 2021 to call Rev. Brian Weinberger as Pastor of Bethlehem Lutheran Church. Pastor Brian began his ministry on August 30, 2021 and was formally installed on October 10, 2021. Together with staff, council, preschool, and volunteers, Bethlehem continues to share God's love and grace in Mesa.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before visiting Bethlehem Lutheran Church.

Is there a dress code?

No, come as you are! Whether you feel most comfortable in casual Arizona clothes, jeans, or Sunday dress, you are welcome exactly as you are.

Is Bethlehem an inclusive community?

Yes! It is very important to us that whoever you are, you know you are welcome now, just as you are. We are a community of grace where all God's children are embraced with open arms.

Is there Communion and can I participate?

We share in Holy Communion every Sunday during worship. Because it is Christ's table, and all are welcome before Christ, we practice open Communion. There are no prerequisites for participating—even if you've never been to church before, you are welcome at God's table.

Is there stuff for kids during Sunday worship?

We love kids at Bethlehem! All children are welcome in the worship service—and they don't have to be quiet! We provide worship activity bags in the narthex filled with coloring books and quiet activities. Every Sunday, children are invited forward for a special Children's sermon and blessing, and we offer child-friendly communion (grape juice and goldfish crackers).

What programs and opportunities are available for youth?

Youth are a vital part of our congregation! Bethlehem hosts an active Tirosh community and partners with regional East Valley Lutheran youth programs to provide safe, fun, and faith-building retreats, service projects, and fellowship for middle school and high school students.

Do I have to be a Christian or Lutheran to come to Bethlehem?

Not at all! Bethlehem is a community of people who are in lots of different places in their spiritual journey. We don't expect our members or guests to have everything figured out. At Bethlehem, you belong before you believe. We only ask that you be open to exploring faith and God's love together with us.

What is a Lutheran?

Bethlehem is a congregation of the Evangelical Lutheran Church in America (ELCA) ↗ and part of the Grand Canyon Synod. Rooted in the Reformation heritage of Martin Luther, our faith is centered on God's unconditional grace through Jesus Christ: we are saved by grace through faith, called to serve our neighbors in love.

Does Bethlehem have a preschool or early childhood program?

Yes! Noah's Ark Preschool & Kindergarten ↗ is an accredited weekday ministry of Bethlehem Lutheran Church, led by Director Jill Majestic. Noah's Ark offers loving Christian early childhood education with extended care hours on our campus.

How do I become a member?

At Bethlehem, we invite you to join our Mission, not just a roster. If you're interested in making Bethlehem your spiritual home, contact Pastor Brian or the church office at office@blcmesa.org or call (480) 832-0462.
