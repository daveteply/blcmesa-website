Welcome to Bethlehem

A community rooted in faith, open to questions, and active in love.

At Bethlehem Lutheran Church, we believe church should be a sanctuary of genuine acceptance. Led by Pastor Brian Weinberger, our congregation gathers to celebrate the grace of Jesus Christ and to serve our neighbors in Mesa and across the Valley.

Whether you are searching for a church home, asking life's deeper questions, or seeking a loving preschool for your child, our doors and hearts are open to you.

Learn More About Our Beliefs

✝️ Core Values of Our Church

  • Grace First: God's love is a gift, unconditional and freely given to all.
  • All Means All: We welcome diversity of background, perspective, and identity.
  • Hands-On Service: Putting faith into action through our Community Garden and food ministry.
  • Lifelong Growth: Nurturing faith from Noah's Ark preschool through senior adult studies.
